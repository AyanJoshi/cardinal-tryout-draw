Tryout Draw

Enter the roster once. Singles, doubles and mixed each have their own pool and their own draw button, so untick anyone sitting out an event and draw that event on its own. Redrawing one event never clears scores in the others. Every match is best of 3 games to 15. Singles is winners-play-winners: round 1 is random, then players with the same record meet, so nobody is knocked out. With an odd number, the lowest-ranked player who hasn't sat out yet gets the bye, which never counts as a win. Doubles and mixed give everyone a new partner each round.

Scores save on this device as you type, so a refresh won't lose them.

The overall ranking picks the team. The Singles, Doubles and Mixed columns help set the lineup: who plays which event in each tie, with no one playing more than 2 matches per tie. A dash means the player wasn't in that event's pool; they're ranked on the matches they did play. Ties at the cutoff: check head-to-head singles first, and have a selector who is not in contention make the call.
